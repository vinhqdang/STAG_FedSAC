\paragraph{Learned DRL Baselines and Stability Analysis} 
STAG-FedSAC decisively outperforms the existing federated and centralized DRL baselines: it improves reward by $+56.9\%$ over FedDDPG ($\mFedDDPGReward$) and by $+68.5\%$ over LSTM-DRL ($\mLSTMDRLReward$), while cutting latency by $40.5\%$ (from $184.8$~ms) and $44.7\%$ (from $198.9$~ms), respectively. Table~\ref{tab:stats} reports the paired difference statistics across matched random seeds; all paired differences against the learned baselines are strictly positive with bootstrap intervals entirely bounded away from zero.

The primary factor driving the poor performance of FedDDPG and LSTM-DRL is \emph{policy instability and channel-switching thrashing}. As shown in Table~\ref{tab:main}, FedDDPG and LSTM-DRL reassign the operating channels of $\mFedDDPGSwitch$ and $\mLSTMDRLSwitch$ APs per control interval, respectively. In a realistic deployment with MAC contention and channel-switch outages (Section~\ref{sec:deploy}), frequent channel changes incur severe service interruption ($t_{\mathrm{sw}}=3$~s) and queuing delays that devastate user throughput and latency. Because standard DDPG lacks entropy regularization and proximal policy constraints, policy updates cause continuous parameter drift away from coordinated frequency plans. 

In contrast, STAG-FedSAC maintains exceptional operational stability ($\mPropSwitch$ switches/AP/step). By leveraging maximum-entropy SAC exploration, personal actor heads, and proximal distillation regularisation (HierFed-KD), STAG-FedSAC avoids erratic channel flipping. Instead, it proactively fine-tunes continuous transmit powers and dynamic EDCA airtime allocations based on upcoming schedule surges, adapting resources smoothly while preserving medium stability.

Figure~\ref{fig:conv} illustrates the convergence trajectories across training episodes. Both DDPG baselines rapidly degrade immediately after the exploration warm-up phase ($200$ episodes), falling into high-latency sub-optimal policies. In contrast, STAG-FedSAC maintains high performance throughout training, steadily optimizing radio parameters and achieving consistent convergence. Furthermore, a perfect-information greedy oracle evaluated on test episodes achieves an upper-bound reward of $\oracleReward$ ($+\oracleGainPct\%$ above SSF), confirming that substantial headroom exists for proactive schedule-aware optimization in dense transit venues.
