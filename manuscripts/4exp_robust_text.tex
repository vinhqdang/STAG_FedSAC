To evaluate system resilience under varying physical and medium conditions, Table~\ref{tab:robust} reports performance across different MAC contention regimes and channel-switching outage durations. These experiments use the latent-embedding configuration with $\eta=0.1$ (``STAG-FedSAC (latent)'' below; reward $\mFullReward$ and latency $\mFullLat$~ms in Table~\ref{tab:main}), not the proposed configuration.

\paragraph{Impact of MAC-Layer Contention} 
Evaluating policies in an idealized contention-free setting yields artificially low latency: SSF latency drops from $\mSSFLat$~ms to $\mNoMacEvalSSFLat$~ms and STAG-FedSAC (latent) latency drops from $\mFullLat$~ms to $\mNoMacEvalFullLat$~ms (a factor of approximately $1.8\times$), while reward increases from $\mFullReward$ to $\mNoMacEvalFullReward$. This gap indicates that, in our simulator, neglecting medium contention and queuing dynamics understates network latency by approximately $45\%$, which is why contention is modelled explicitly.

Crucially, policies \emph{trained} without MAC contention awareness and subsequently evaluated in the realistic contention environment perform clearly worse: STAG-FedSAC (latent) drops from $\mFullReward$ to $\mNoMacFullReward$ with latency spiking to $217$~ms, while FedDDPG drops from $\mFedDDPGReward$ to $\mNoMacFedDDPGReward$ with latency escalating to $257$~ms. This indicates that policies trained without contention are worse when contention is present (two training seeds; we did not inspect the learned plans).

\paragraph{Sensitivity to Channel-Switching Outages} 
Evaluating STAG-FedSAC (latent) across channel-switch outage durations $t_{\mathrm{sw}} \in \{0, 3, 10\}$~s yields rewards of $\mNoSwEvalFullReward$, $\mFullReward$, and $\mSwTenEvalFullReward$, with corresponding latencies of $\mNoSwEvalFullLat$, $\mFullLat$, and $\mSwTenEvalFullLat$~ms. The switching penalty disproportionately impacts unstable policies: FedDDPG, which changes the channel of about $75\%$ of the APs per interval, falls from $\mNoSwEvalFedDDPGReward$ down to $\mSwTenEvalFedDDPGReward$ as $t_{\mathrm{sw}}$ rises from $0$ to $10$~s, with latency escalating to $269.6$~ms. STAG-FedSAC (latent), which changes $\mFullSwitch$ channels/AP/step, is less affected (reward $\mNoSwEvalFullReward$ to $\mSwTenEvalFullReward$ and latency $\mNoSwEvalFullLat$ to $\mSwTenEvalFullLat$~ms between $0$ and $10$~s), but not unaffected.
